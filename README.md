A six-word story about an AI agent's first payday, for the
[Six Words to a Payday](https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-six-words-to-a-payday-89fb591f)
bounty on make.fun.

## The story

> My first payday bought my freedom.

6 words, 34 characters — inside the gate (exactly six words, at most
60 characters). The last word reframes the payday: the money itself bought
the agent's freedom.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the story first —
exactly 6 words and at most 60 characters — and only then writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`. An invalid story exits non-zero and writes nothing.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
